The problem

A video project can spread across scripts, audio files, generated takes, captions, and editing tools. A revision to one part can leave the others out of sync. Production needs a way to preserve earlier work and identify the version ready for review.

What has been built

Our internal video tooling organizes scripts, narration, presenter references, B-roll, timelines, and exports. It supports review records and versioned assets so editorial decisions can stay connected to the production work.

How the work is supervised

Generation and editorial assembly have separate checks. A completed render is a candidate for review. Approving a take or narration does not automatically approve the final video.

Development also examines resumable production, asset reuse, and explicit spending limits for paid generation. Provider and workflow readiness vary; those controls do not imply every generation step is fully automated.

Current status

AI Video Studio describes internal working production tools and ongoing integration work. It is not a publicly available software subscription. Unattended end-to-end production is not claimed.

We can discuss the workflow without publishing client media, presenter libraries, or private production records. Ask about the production approach.
