The problem

A dealership inquiry can involve vehicle questions, contact information, appointment preferences, and a request for staff help. The next action needs a clear owner, and the record needs to match what actually happened.

What is being developed

BellRinger is being built around a bounded set of dealership sales voice workflows. The intended system captures relevant context, follows an approved disposition, and supports a clear handoff to dealership staff.

The engineering focus

The development work separates the conversation from the rules governing its next action. A local record, a requested external action, and a verified result are treated as distinct states.

Workflow-specific review and activation decisions remain necessary before live use. Intended appointment and follow-up behavior should not be read as a claim of a currently activated dealership service.

Current status

BellRinger is in development for a controlled pilot. This page does not claim a completed paid pilot, general commercial availability, or any particular live CRM integration.

For product inquiries or a conversation about the pilot’s current readiness, contact Abide Labs about BellRinger.
