About Abide Labs

Abide Labs is an independent technology company developing its own products and exploring practical applications of AI. Our work connects software engineering with the details of how people actually use a system.

We are building toward more useful AI applications and more dependable ways to coordinate them. That means testing a small, meaningful use case before expanding its scope.
