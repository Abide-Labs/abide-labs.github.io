AI-powered applications

We develop the software around an AI interaction: the interface, the data it needs, the permitted next steps, and the record of what happened. BellRinger applies this approach to dealership voice workflows under development.

Relevant work: BellRinger project overview.

Business workflow automation

Intake, follow-up, scheduling, and internal administration are useful starting points when their inputs and responsibilities are clear. We examine the existing process, define where software can help, and identify decisions that should stay with people.

Integration readiness is specific to each project. We do not treat a local test as evidence that a live customer system is connected.

Generative AI production systems

Our video work connects narration, fictional presenters, B-roll, captions, and timeline editing. The development challenge is keeping the script, selected assets, revisions, and review decisions consistent through an export.

Relevant work: AI Video Studio workflow.

Multi-agent orchestration & governance

Our development tooling explores how to assign bounded tasks, carry context through handoffs, and record evidence before treating work as complete. Human approval and limits on an agent’s authority are part of that operating approach.

This is an internal engineering practice and an area of continued experimentation. It is not offered here as a standalone commercial orchestration platform.

Product experiments

ReaderRealms explores authored interactive storytelling. Gumbo examines evidence-linked veterinary care discovery. Both help us investigate product questions in a limited prototype before making broader promises.

ReaderRealms · Gumbo

Discussing a possible build

We welcome conversations with business operators, product teams, and creative collaborators who can describe a concrete need. The first question is what a useful result would look like and how we could verify it.
